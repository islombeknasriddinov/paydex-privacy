# Paydex — privacy policy

The privacy policy for the Android app **Paydex**
(`uz.nasriddinovislombek.paydex`), published on Google Play by
Islombek Nasriddinov.

Published at <https://islombeknasriddinov.github.io/paydex-privacy/>, which
is the URL given in Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.

`index.html` is generated from `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` in the app
repository by `tools/build_privacy_page.py`; edit the policy there, rebuild,
and commit the result here so the published page cannot drift from what the
app does.
